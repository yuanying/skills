# Engineering Writing

## Preserve the artifact's contract

Follow required templates, sections, and format-specific workflows. Apply this guidance within them; do not replace them or impose a fixed structure.

## Keep durable content

- Omit implementation chatter, temporary uncertainty, abandoned wording, review back-and-forth, and resolved questions unless they leave a lasting constraint, risk, decision, or follow-up.
- Remove repetition and detail that does not change the reader's understanding, decision, risk assessment, or follow-up.
- Include relevant background, the problem, outcome or proposal, constraints, consequential trade-offs, impact, risks, verification, and remaining obligations.
- Record a rejected alternative only when its rejection explains a durable decision or prevents likely re-litigation.

## Write for third parties

- Supply the minimum background and rationale needed without the session context.
- Replace references such as "as discussed" and implementation chronology with direct context. Organize around what the reader must understand or decide.
- Do not expose private chain-of-thought or internal agent reasoning; state relevant conclusions, evidence, and rationale directly.
